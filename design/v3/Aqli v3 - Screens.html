19 · RetiredThe four routes v3 deletes, and where their job went.

Four fewer places to be.

Each of these was a destination in the old app. None of them was where anyone wanted to be — they were all detours around the same question: is this doc still true?

/stale

“Needs updating” — a whole page listing docs that had gone quiet.

The Ageing state

Carried by the doc itself, everywhere it appears — list, search, sidebar, phone. Plus one filter chip on the space page.

/agent-log

A feed of everything the AI had done, which nobody read twice.

Settings › AI access

Per-agent counts on the key that produced them. The drafts themselves arrive in Checks, where they can be acted on.

/review

“Review Queue”, sitting under a sidebar group called Workflow.

Checks

Same job, human words, top-level. The Workflow group is gone — it was navigation for a process, not for people.

/s/[space]/new

Pick a doc type, pick a space, name it — three decisions before a single word.

Deleted

Write goes straight to a cursor. Type and space are asked once, in the Publish sheet, when the answers are actually known.
